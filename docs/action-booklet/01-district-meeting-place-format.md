# District Meeting Place & Format

## November 1983

Decided to update meeting schedules and get contributions from the groups to do this.

## December 1983

The District meeting is to be the first week of the month & rotate the days of the week.

## January 1984

This year rather than have concepts as topics at District meeting, different topics pertinent to the GSR’s role in
AA will be discussed.

## May 1984

A $5.00 service fee will be returned to home groups in the District who host district meetings.

## February 1985

District approved a change in meeting format as follows: DCM report first followed by old & new business,
then standing committee reports and GSR reports last.

## March 1986

It was decided to have the PI, CPC and Grapevine committee meetings a 6:30 p.m., prior to the District
meeting.

## August 1987

Motion a made to have the host GSR only presents the topic at the district meeting.

## December 1988

Motion passed to keep district meeting scheduled the way they are.

## February 1990

The Area #35 Delegate’s report will be given in conjunction with District 9 & 14.

## March 1990

Decided to have Area #35 Delegate’s report in this District alone.

## May 1990

No GSR reports given because of Area #35 Delegate’s report.

## July 1990

District supported that the Area Delegate gives his report at one location in each of the 4 zones of Area #35.

## August 1990

Motion carried that the district will pay the prevailing rental cost for holding a meeting provided there is
advance notice of what the cost will be. Rationale was that the $5.00 does not cover the entire cost of some of
the meeting places.

## January 1994

Group conscience to continue rotating meeting location instead of having one central meeting place.

## November 1996

DCM will contact host groups contact person (usually GSR) and local radio stations to cancel District
meeting in the event of bad weather.

## April 1997

Motion made to dispense with the GSR reports at our next meeting to allow more time for our Delegates
report. Passed.

## December 1997

Passed to discuss at January meeting that new GSR’s come out of the hat and go on action committees.

## January 1998

Passed to go back to the groups with another vote at February district meeting for GSR’s names to go in hat
and put on action committees.

## February 1998

Passed for GSR’s not to go to the hat and be put on action committees.

## November 1998

Motion made and approved to change the March 1999 district meeting to March 10, 1999 due to a conflict
with the Winter Rap-Up held the same day.

## April 2002

Motion made and seconded to follow old format at the next business meeting to allow for the Delegate to report on the 2002 General Service Conference. All District Officers and GSR’s are to submit written report.

## January 2004
