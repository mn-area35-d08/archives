# District Officers

## January 1986

Motion carried to have the secretary make 25 extra copies of
each months' minutes to be shared with area officers and other districts.

## April 1986

Motion carried to have DCM send postcards as reminders of District meetings.

## January 1990

Group Conscience Decision: Motion made that if officers miss two consecutive meetings, they are talked to.
If three are missed, find a permanent replacement for them.

## March 1992

Motion to include the Alt. DCM along with the Committee Chairs and DCM (travel expenses).

## April 1992

Motion to have the treasurer prepare a forecast based on the last two years of expenses.

## July 1992

Motion that said, “In case of emergency, when the Action Committee Chair can’t go, and the Alt. Can’t go;
the Action Committee Chair can appoint someone to go in his place, but should to try and get district
approval.”

## August 1992

Motion to cancel out the motion of July 10th, that said Action Committee Chairs could appoint who would go
in there place (Passed Unanimously).

## January 1994

Motion passed to have District Secretary make up and sends a new District Officer/GSR mailing addresses
once during their term.

## January 1994

Motion to have District Secretary do District Action Booklet update in the second year of their term.

## March 1994

Motion passed for district to have Archives Chair.

## June 2010

Elected Alternate DCM by method of “Going to the Hat” after five votes were taken and
no candidate received 2/3 of the votes.

## July 2010

Elected District 8 Treasurer using the Third Legacy Procedure.

## December 2013

At the beginning of each rotation $50.00 each for the DCM,Treasurer, and Secretary for supplies is approved
for supplies needed. Any unused monies to be returned to the District.

## May 2014

Motion passed regarding Alternate Committee Chairs attendance. First meeting missed the person calls the
DCM to be excused. Second time missed DCM calls the Alternate. The third time missed the body makes the
decision whether to dismiss them from their position or not.
