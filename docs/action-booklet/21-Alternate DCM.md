# ALTERNATE DCM TRUSTED SERVANT GUIDELINES
District 8

Updated February 2024

## Position Description:

•	Support the DCM and be ready to step in if the DCM cannot attend an Area Committee Meeting (ACM), Assembly, Area Workshop, West Central Regional A.A. Service Con-ference, or any other meeting where needed.
•	Be prepared to assume all DCM duties if the DCM is unable to fulfill his/her duties.
•	Assist the DCM in visiting groups when requested.
•	Organize service workshops for the District


## Responsibilities:

•	Attend all District Meetings.
•	Give reports of all activities and of all meetings attended.
•	Be prudent with spending, in order to minimize expenses to the District.
•	Submit for reimbursement of expenses in a timely manner.
•	Obtain, read, and use the Service Manual and any GSO-related materials.
•	Update the Trusted Servant Guidelines and submit the updates by June of the second year to the District Secretary.
•	Assist the incoming Alternate DCM with experience, records, information, and sugges-tions.
