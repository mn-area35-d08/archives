# TREASURER Trusted Servant Guidelines

District 8

Updated February 2024

## Position Description:

•	Organize the finances of the District and create awareness of the Seventh Tradition.

•	Keep an eye on the District’s solvency and maintain a prudent reserve.

## Responsibilities:

•	Read the A.A. Service Manual.

•	Provide a detailed monthly report of the contributions and expenditures at each District meeting.

•	Give reports of activities and all meetings attended.

•	Make timely deposits to the checking account.

•	Send receipts to the groups for their contributions.

•	Compile an annual report at the end of each year and give a copy to the Archivist.  Also make copies available to all members who want them.

•	Ensure expenditures are minimized while providing the required services.

•	Inform groups of the need to support the District, Area 35, and G.S.O.

•	Assist incoming Treasurer with experience, records, information, and suggestions.

•	Update the Trusted Servant Guidelines and submit updates by June of the second year to the District Secretary.
