# Cooperation with Professional Community

## December 1985

Area elected a CPC chairperson and requested a committee composed of district CPC chair people.
District 8 elected CPC chair.

## February 1986

Motion failed to have a co-chair for CPC. Majority felt there was no precedent with other committees and it
could end up doubling expenses. Minority opinion felt there were not enough groups present to settle the issue
and that the area would be better served with two people.

## August 1991

Motion failed to combine CPC and PI chairs.

## December 1991

Motion carried to combine CPC and PI chairs on trial basis only and temporary.
