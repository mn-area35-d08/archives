# SECRETARY Trusted Servant Guidelines
District 8

Updated February 2024

## Position Description:

•	Keep records (Minutes) of all District meetings

## Responsibilities:

•	Read the A.A. Service Manual.

•	Provide Minutes via email to all GSRs, Committee Chairs and Officers, and to any member who requests a copy.  Provide printed copies to any member who requests one and have copies at all District meetings.

•	Give reports of activities and all meetings attended.

•	Provided updated Contact Lists to all members of the District Committees via email.

•	Have Group Information Change Forms available at all District meetings.

•	Remind groups to provide any changes to group information, including changes to con-tacts, group names, meeting locations, and times and days of the week.

•	Work with Area 35 Group Records. Chair to keep group information up to date – grouprecords@area35.org

•	Assist DCM. By making forms, etc., as requested.

•	Update Past Action Booklet at the end of the rotation, email a copy to the Area 35 Ar-chivist, and have as many copies made and bound as the District approves. 

•	Submit receipts to the Treasurer in a timely manner.

•	Send copies of approved Minutes to: secretary@area35.org 

•	archives@area35.org  areachair@area35.org  delegate@area35.org

•	Assist the incoming Secretary with experience, records, information, and suggestions, especially on how to most efficiently update the Past Action Booklet.

•	Don’t be afraid to ask for assistance.
