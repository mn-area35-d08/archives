# Forwards

## First Foreword

This work is the combined effort of many members of the 1992 District committee. We did our best, but it may not be flawless. It should be noted that most of the facts entered here are motions that passed, or are January 1983 to August 1992. It is the hope of everyone involved with this project that this booklet will consensuses that are actions. Failed motions were omitted. The period of recapitulation of minutes is from give future committees a sense of what the policies have been. Not knowing that has been a problem in the past.

## Second Foreword

This is the second edition of the District Action Booklet. It covers the periods from August 1992 to February 1995. The committee was formed to put this together by a district action. It was thought that it would be easier to update the booklet approximately every two years. The committee left the original booklet contents untouched. The committee did it's best to update the booklet contents, but it may not be flawless. Most failed motions were omitted, but a few were included to show the districts Groups feelings on certain issues. The committee decided to change from the original small booklet form to this larger booklet from to help ease the cost of printing.

## Eighth Foreword

This is the eighth edition of the District 8 Action Booklet. It includes actions from January 2014 to December 2015. The District Action Booklet committee made a change to the format of the booklet. The Corrections and Treatment section was divided into two separate sections, as they now represent separate committee chairs within the District. All Treatment actions are now found in the Treatment section and all Corrections actions are found in the Corrections section.
